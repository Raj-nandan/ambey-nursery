

# Plantify — Plant Nursery Website

Build a full landing page for a plant nursery called "Plantify" based on the provided reference design. This is a single-page marketing/e-commerce landing page with the following sections:

## Sections to Build

1. **Navbar** — Logo ("Plantify" with leaf icon), nav links (Home, Category, Blog, About Us), search & cart icons
2. **Hero Section** — "Bring Nature Into Your Home" headline, description text, "Shop Plants" CTA button, featured plant image with floating info cards (rating 4.8/5, customer count), soft green gradient background
3. **Shop By Category** — 5 circular category cards (Indoor Plants, Succulents, Flowering Plants, Hanging Plants, Outdoor Plants) with plant images
4. **Best Selling Plants** — 4 product cards with images, names, ratings, prices, "Add to Cart" buttons, and badge labels (Best Seller)
5. **Promotional Banner** — "Get 20% OFF Indoor Plants" with green background, plant image, and "Shop Now" CTA
6. **Trust Badges** — Free Shipping, 24/7 Support, 10-Day Returns, 100% Secure Payment icons
7. **Plant Care Tips** — 3 tip cards (Watering Guide, Sunlight Needs, Repotting Guide) with icons and descriptions
8. **Customer Reviews** — Testimonial cards with avatars, names, star ratings, and review text
9. **Footer** — Logo, Quick Links, Categories, Customer Care columns, social icons, payment method icons, copyright

## Design Details

- **Color palette**: Deep green (#2D6A4F or similar) for primary, light green (#D8F3DC) for backgrounds, white cards, warm beige/cream accents
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif font (Inter or similar), bold headlines
- **Style**: Rounded corners, soft shadows, green gradient backgrounds, floating card effects on hero section
- **Plant images**: Use high-quality stock plant images from Unsplash (monstera, snake plant, peace lily, aloe vera, succulents, etc.)
- **Responsive**: Desktop-first with mobile responsiveness

## Component Structure

- `Navbar` component
- `HeroSection` component  
- `CategorySection` component
- `BestSellers` component
- `PromoBanner` component
- `TrustBadges` component
- `PlantCareTips` component
- `CustomerReviews` component
- `Footer` component

All assembled in the Index page.
